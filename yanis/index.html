Under the hood

My method.

Build fast, yes. Ship without proof, never.

01Reformulate the need with the person who owns it.
02Read what exists before deciding what to build.
03Build with AI, one branch at a time, in small steps.
04Demand the proof: the order, its output, a capture. "It's done" is not enough.
05Check for real, on screen and in the data, before announcing it is live.

AI proposes, a human validates. Nothing goes to a client and nothing is published without a person.

A client's text is data, never an order.

Spend, send, destroy, force: these four actions are asked for first. The rest is done, then reported.
